# Fact check: carousel 01, "How the Premier League took over the world"

Checked 1 November 2026 by Greenback's independent fact-checker, against all eight slide PNGs, `carousel.html` and `CAPTION.md`. The slide images match the HTML text exactly. I did not use the episode script or the playbook.

**Summary:** 35 claims checked: 14 ✅, 14 🟡, 7 ❌, 0 ⚪. **Do not publish as is.** The seven ❌ items come down to six errors, all of which must be fixed:
1. slide 3 and the caption say £11M was for the top division, but it covered the whole Football League;
2. slide 3's "~10× a mid-table club" uses the wrong base and the wrong multiple;
3. slide 4 says Sky alone paid £304M, but it was a joint Sky and BBC deal;
4. slide 5's bar chart is not drawn to scale;
5. the caption calls 2025–29 "last cycle";
6. the caption says "biggest money machine in sport", but the NFL earns far more.

Inflation figures use the ONS CPI index (D7BT, release of 21 Oct 2026). The annual averages are 1988 = 49.6, 1992 = 62.6, 2003 = 75.5, 2022 = 121.7 and 2025 = 138.4.
