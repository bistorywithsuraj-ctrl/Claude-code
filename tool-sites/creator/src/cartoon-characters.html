How to make the cartoon character trend

Pick a clip with some open ground or sky, like a field, a street or a beach. Add two or three characters, spread them out, and give each one a different move. Put their feet on the ground line, turn on "Feet in the grass" for grass or sand, and keep color match around 60 to 70 so they pick up the light of the scene. Export, then add a trending sound in Instagram or YouTube.

Questions

Can I use these characters in monetised videos?

Yes. The characters are original Creator Bench designs drawn by code, and you can use the videos you make anywhere, including monetised YouTube, Reels and client work.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

How long can my video be?

Up to 60 seconds, which covers Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Longer clips are trimmed to the first minute.

Why does export take a while?

Every frame is drawn and encoded one by one so nothing stutters. A 15-second 1080p clip usually takes under a minute on a laptop; phones are slower.
