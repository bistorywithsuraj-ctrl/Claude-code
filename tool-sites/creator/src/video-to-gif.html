Make a GIF from a video

  1. Upload a video.
  2. Pick the start and end (shorter GIFs look better and stay small).
  3. Choose frame rate (8–20 fps) and width (320–800 px), then download.

Tips

  • 2–4 seconds is ideal for reactions and memes.
  • Use 12–15 fps for a good balance of smoothness and size.
  • GIFs have no sound; for clips with audio, keep them as MP4.

Questions

How do I make my GIF smaller?

Shorten it, lower the frame rate to 8 or 12, or pick a smaller width. Each of these cuts the file size a lot.

Which videos work?

MP4, MOV and WebM files that your browser can play. iPhone HEVC videos work in Safari; in other browsers, export them as MP4 first.

Why is my GIF so big?

GIF is an old format. Lower the frame rate, the width or the length to shrink it.
