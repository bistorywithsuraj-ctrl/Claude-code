Style

How the match cut works

A match cut keeps one element in the same place while everything around it changes. Here your word stays in exactly the same spot and size while each front page has a different typeface, paper, layout and photo, so it looks like the whole world is printing your headline. Every page is drawn fresh in your browser, and the newspaper names are made up.

How to make the newspaper match cut trend

  1. Type your sentence, for example "ChatGPT is everywhere" or your channel's catchphrase.
  2. Pick a look: Article close-up (the trending style) or Front page.
  3. Choose 9:16 for Reels and Shorts or 16:9 for YouTube.
  4. Set speed and resolution, then export. The paper-slap sound is already synced to every cut.
  5. Add it to the start of your video as a hook, or post it alone with a trending sound.

Ideas that work

  • A bold opinion: "Nobody talks about this."
  • A product or brand name repeated across headlines.
  • News commentary and explainer intros.
  • Hindi headlines, which look striking because few people make them.

Tips

  • Keep it under 8 words. The effect is strongest when each word lands fast.
  • Use your handle in the overlay so reposts still credit you.
  • Export in 1080p for Instagram; 4K only matters for YouTube.

Questions

How long is the video?

About one second per word at Fast speed, plus the final page. The time appears next to the Play button.

Can I use it in monetised videos?

Yes. The pages are generated by the tool with open-source fonts and invented newspaper names, and there is no watermark.

Which resolution should I pick?

1080p is right for Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Pick 4K for YouTube or if you will zoom and crop in your editor. Every frame is rendered one by one, so nothing is dropped even at 4K; higher resolutions just take longer to export.

Is this the same effect as the viral ChatGPT newspaper reel?

Yes, it creates the same style: each word appears on a different newspaper or article page with fast cuts and a paper sound.

Do I need After Effects?

No. Everything is generated in your browser in seconds.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. The pages are generated from scratch, with no real newspaper logos, so you can use them in any video.
