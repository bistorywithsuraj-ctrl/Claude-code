How it works

Your browser decodes the video's soundtrack and encodes it to MP3 with the open-source LAME encoder, all on your device. Nothing is uploaded, so it is private and there is no file size limit beyond your computer's memory.

Questions

Which bitrate should I pick?

192 kbps is right for most voiceovers and music. Choose 128 kbps for smaller files such as voice notes, or 320 kbps when you will edit the audio again.

Can I convert Instagram or YouTube videos?

Only videos saved on your own device. Use your own content or audio you have permission to use.

Why use WAV?

WAV is lossless, so it is best for editing in apps like CapCut, Premiere or Audacity. The file is about ten times larger than an MP3.
