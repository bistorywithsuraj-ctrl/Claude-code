How we count

Words are groups of letters or numbers separated by spaces. Characters include spaces, punctuation and line breaks, the way most platforms count them; emoji count as one character here, although some apps count certain emoji as two. Reading time assumes 238 words per minute and speaking time 150.

Questions

What is the YouTube title and description limit?

Titles can be 100 characters and descriptions 5,000 characters. Only about the first 60 characters of a title show in most places.

What is the Instagram caption limit?

2,200 characters for captions and 150 characters for your bio.

How long can a TikTok caption be?

Up to 4,000 characters.

How many characters can a post on X have?

280 characters for standard accounts. Paid accounts can post longer.

Is my text saved?

Only in your own browser, so it's still there if you come back. It's never sent to a server.
