How many hashtags should you use?

Fewer, more relevant hashtags beat a wall of popular ones. A good mix is one or two broad tags for reach, a few medium tags, and several niche tags where your video can actually rank. Platforms change their guidance often: Instagram currently suggests a handful of relevant tags, TikTok works well with three to five, and YouTube shows the first three hashtags above your title.

How many hashtags should I use?

A focused mix works better than 30 random tags. Combine a few broad tags, several medium ones and a few niche tags that describe exactly what your video is about.

How to use it

  1. Pick the platform: Reels, TikTok or Shorts.
  2. Choose your niche and type your topic.
  3. Pick how many (10–30) and copy the balanced set.

Tips

  • Hashtags help categorise your video; the hook and watch time matter much more.
  • Add 1–2 Hindi or regional tags for Indian audiences, like #hinditips.

Questions

Do hashtags still work in 2026?

They help platforms understand what your video is about and who to show it to. They will not make a weak video go viral, but the right niche tags help the right people find you.

Should I use the same hashtags on every post?

Keep a core of niche tags that describe your channel, and change the topic tags for each video. Press Shuffle for a fresh mix.

Do hashtags still work on Instagram?

They help Instagram understand your topic, but they are a small factor compared with content quality and watch time.
