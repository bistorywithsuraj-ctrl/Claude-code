Questions

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

1280 × 720 pixels (16:9), under 2 MB. This tool always exports at exactly that size; choose JPG if the PNG is over 2 MB.

How much text should a thumbnail have?

Three to five big words. Thumbnails are seen small on phones, so short, high-contrast text with one highlighted word reads best.
