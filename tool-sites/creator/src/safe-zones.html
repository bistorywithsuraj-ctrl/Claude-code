Questions

What size should a Short, Reel or TikTok be?

1080 × 1920 pixels, a 9:16 vertical frame. All three apps crop or letterbox other shapes.

Where should I put text in a vertical video?

In the middle of the frame, away from the bottom fifth and the right edge. Text placed low is usually covered by the caption and username.

Can I make one video that works on all three apps?

Yes. Choose "All three" to see the combined safe area, then keep everything important inside it.

Is my video uploaded?

No. It plays in your browser only and never leaves your device.
