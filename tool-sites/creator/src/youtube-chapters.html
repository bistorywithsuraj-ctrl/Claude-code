YouTube's chapter rules

  • The first timestamp must be 0:00.
  • You need at least three timestamps, in order.
  • Each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long.
  • Put the list in your video description, one chapter per line.

If any rule is broken, YouTube quietly ignores all of your chapters. This tool checks each rule and tells you what to change.

Questions

Why aren't my YouTube chapters showing?

Usually because the first timestamp isn't 0:00, there are fewer than three, one chapter is shorter than 10 seconds, or automatic chapters were turned off. Paste yours in "Check my timestamps" to find the problem.

Where do chapters go?

In the video description, as a list of timestamps followed by titles. They can be anywhere in the description, but most creators put them near the top.

Do chapters help my video rank?

They can appear as "key moments" in Google search and help viewers find what they want, which often improves watch time.
