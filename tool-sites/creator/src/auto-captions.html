How it works

Creator Bench runs OpenAI's open-source Whisper speech model inside your browser. The first time, your browser downloads the model (40 or 80 MB) and keeps it for next time. Your video never leaves your device: transcription, styling and export all happen locally.

Word-level timing lets every caption style animate word by word, the way viral Reels and Shorts look. Exporting plays the video once and records it with the captions, so a 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds.

Questions

Are these auto captions really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on video length. Because the AI runs on your own device, there is no server cost to pass on.

Does it work for Hindi and Hinglish?

Choose "Accurate · 99 languages" and pick Hindi, or leave it on automatic detection. Hinglish speech is usually written in Latin or Devanagari script depending on what the model hears; you can edit any line afterwards.

How do I add the captions to Instagram or YouTube?

For Reels and TikTok, export the captioned video and upload it. For YouTube, download the SRT file and add it under Subtitles in YouTube Studio, or upload the captioned video.

Why is the first run slow?

The AI model has to download once. After that it loads from your browser's cache in a few seconds. A phone works, but a computer is much faster.
