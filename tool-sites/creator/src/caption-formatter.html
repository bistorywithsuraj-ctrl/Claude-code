How the line-break fix works

Instagram sometimes deletes empty lines, squashing your paragraphs together. We put an invisible blank character on each empty line, so the gap survives but nothing shows. Each one counts as a single character toward the limit.

In the feed, Instagram shows roughly the first 125 characters (or the first couple of lines) and hides the rest behind "… more". Put your hook there.

Questions

What is the Instagram caption limit?

2,200 characters, including spaces, emoji, line breaks and hashtags.

How many hashtags can I use?

Up to 30 per post. Instagram itself suggests 3 to 5 relevant ones work best.

Why do my line breaks disappear?

Instagram can strip lines that contain nothing, including lines ending in a space. Formatting the caption here adds an invisible character so each blank line is kept.

Does this work for TikTok and Threads?

The formatting works anywhere you paste it. The limits shown here are Instagram's: TikTok allows 4,000 characters and Threads 500.

Is my caption saved anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so it's still there if you come back. It's never sent to a server.
