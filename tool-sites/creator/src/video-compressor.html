How it works

Your browser plays the video once and records it again at the size and bitrate you choose, so a one-minute video takes about a minute. Keep this tab open while it converts. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no watermark.

Compress a video for WhatsApp, email or Discord

Pick a target (WhatsApp under 16 MB, email under 25 MB, Discord under 8 MB) or your own quality and resolution, and the tool picks the right bitrate. You can also turn a 16:9 video into 9:16 with a blurred background for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Tips

  • 720p is plenty for phones and saves a lot of space.
  • Shorter clips keep more quality at the same size.
  • Convert landscape YouTube clips to 9:16 to repurpose them as Shorts.

Questions

How do I turn a YouTube video into a Reel?

Choose "9:16 blur fill": your full landscape video sits in the middle of a vertical frame with a soft blurred copy behind it. Choose "9:16 crop" to fill the screen by cutting the sides.

Why is the output WebM?

Some browsers can only record WebM. Chrome, Edge and Safari on recent versions save MP4. YouTube, TikTok and WhatsApp accept both.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens in your browser.
