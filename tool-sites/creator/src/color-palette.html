Questions

How are the colors picked?

The image is shrunk and its pixels are grouped into clusters of similar color (k-means). Each swatch is the average of one cluster, sorted from the most to the least common.

What does the Aa on each swatch mean?

It shows whether black or white text is easier to read on that color, so you can pick text colors for thumbnails that pass contrast checks.

What is a HEX code?

A six-character colour code like #E5157A that design apps, websites and editors understand.
