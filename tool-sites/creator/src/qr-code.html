A QR code that never expires

Make a QR code for your channel link, a message or your WiFi. It works forever, because the information is stored in the code itself, not on our server.

What should it open?

Your QR code

Test it with your phone camera before printing. Keep strong contrast: dark code on a light background scans best.

How these QR codes work

The link, text or WiFi details are stored inside the pattern itself. That means the code works offline, never expires and doesn't pass through any tracking service. If you change your link later, you need to make a new code.

Higher error correction lets the code scan even when part of it is damaged or covered, at the cost of a denser pattern.

Questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. They contain your information directly and don't depend on our website, so they keep working forever.

Can I use them commercially?

Yes. Print them on merch, business cards, video end screens or packaging.

PNG or SVG?

PNG for social media and documents, SVG for printing at any size without blur.

How does the WiFi QR code work?

When someone scans it, their phone offers to join your network without typing the password. The password is only stored in the code, not on our site.
