How it works

Text to SRT splits your script into short subtitle cards of up to two lines, then times each card from its word count and your speaking pace, with a small gap between cards. Upload the file to YouTube, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or CapCut, and nudge any card that needs it.

Fix SRT timing moves every subtitle by the same amount. If subtitles appear too late, use a negative number. The speed factor stretches the timing to fix drift between different frame rates.

Questions

What is an SRT file?

A plain-text subtitle file: a number, a start and end time, and the caption text for each line. Almost every video platform and editor accepts it.

How do I add SRT subtitles to YouTube?

In YouTube Studio open the video, choose Subtitles, add a language, then "Upload file" and pick "With timing".

My subtitles slowly drift out of sync. What do I do?

That's usually a frame-rate mismatch. Try a speed factor of 1.001 or 0.999 along with a small shift.

Is my script uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
