How to turn a prompt into a video

  
1. Copy a prompt and paste it into Claude or ChatGPT. 2. Save the reply as a file ending in .html and open it in your browser. 3. Record the screen (on a phone, use the built-in screen recorder; on a Mac, press Shift + Command + 5). 4. Trim it, add music, and add captions with Auto Captions. Change any words, colors or timing by asking the AI in plain English.
