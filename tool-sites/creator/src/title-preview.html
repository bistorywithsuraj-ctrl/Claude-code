Why previewing matters

Viewers decide in a split second, and most of them are on a phone. A title that reads perfectly in your editor can lose its key word behind "…". This preview sets your title at the same font sizes and widths YouTube uses, so the cut-off lands where it really will.

The neighbouring "ghost" videos are there so you can judge whether your thumbnail stands out in a busy feed.

Questions

How long can a YouTube title be?

Up to 100 characters. Only the first 50 to 70 are usually visible before YouTube cuts the title off, depending on where it appears.

Why does the cut-off change with the same character count?

Wide letters like W and M take more room than narrow ones like i and l. That's why this tool measures the title at real font sizes rather than just counting characters.

Is my thumbnail uploaded?

No. The image is read in your browser and never leaves your device.

Is this tool made by YouTube?

No. It's an independent tool that imitates the layout so you can check your designs. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.
