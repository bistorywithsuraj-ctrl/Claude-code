How it works

Creator Bench runs Kokoro, an open-source text-to-speech model released under the Apache 2.0 licence, directly in your browser. Your script is never sent anywhere. Long scripts are split into sentences and joined into one audio file.

Questions

Can I use the voiceover in monetised YouTube videos?

Yes. The Kokoro model is Apache 2.0 licensed, which allows commercial use, and the audio you generate is yours. Disclose AI voices where a platform asks you to.

How long can the script be?

There is no hard limit. A laptop generates roughly a minute of audio in under a minute; phones are slower. Very long scripts are best done in parts.

Does it speak Hindi?

This version speaks English with American and British voices. Write Hindi words in English letters for the best result.
