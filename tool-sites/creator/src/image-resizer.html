Compression

YouTube thumbnails must be under 2 MB. Set a max size and we lower the quality until it fits.

How resizing and compression work

"Fill & crop" scales your image until the whole frame is covered, then trims the edges, which is what you want for thumbnails. "Fit with background" keeps the whole image and fills the empty space with a colour, useful for logos or screenshots.

Compression lowers the image quality slightly to make the file much smaller. At 80–85% the difference is hard to see, but the file is often a third of the size. Everything happens in your browser.

Questions

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

1280 × 720 pixels (16:9), under 2 MB, as JPG, PNG or GIF.

What size is an Instagram post?

1080 × 1080 for square, 1080 × 1350 for portrait, which takes up more of the feed. Stories and Reels are 1080 × 1920.

JPG, PNG or WebP?

JPG for photos and thumbnails, PNG for graphics with sharp edges or transparency, WebP for the smallest files where it's supported.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing and compression happen on your device and your image is never sent anywhere.
