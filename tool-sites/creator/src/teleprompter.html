How to use the online teleprompter

  1. Paste your script.
  2. Set the scroll speed and text size.
  3. Turn on the camera to record yourself behind the text, or mirror the text for a glass teleprompter rig.

Tips to sound natural

  • Place the text close to the camera lens so your eyes look at viewers.
  • Start slower than you think, then speed up.
  • Write in short lines; time your script first with the Script Timer.

Questions

How fast should a teleprompter scroll?

Most people read comfortably at speed 3 to 4, which is close to a natural speaking pace of 140 to 160 words per minute. Adjust it while it runs with the + and − buttons or the arrow keys.

Can I record myself while reading?

Yes. Turn on the camera, then press Record video. The camera shows faintly behind the text so you can keep your eyes near the lens. The recording downloads to your device when you stop.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Prop your phone below your camera lens, open this page, and tap Full screen.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On a phone, prop it near your camera, or use a laptop with its webcam.
