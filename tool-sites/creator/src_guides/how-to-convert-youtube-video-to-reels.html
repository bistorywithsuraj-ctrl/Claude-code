Repurposing your best YouTube moments as Reels and Shorts is one of the fastest ways to grow. The catch is the shape: YouTube videos are wide, and Reels are tall.

Two ways to reframe

  • Blur fill. Your whole wide video sits in the middle of a vertical frame, with a soft blurred copy filling the top and bottom. Nothing gets cut off, which is great for screen recordings, maps and gameplay.
  • Centre crop. The sides are cut so the video fills the screen. Best when the subject is in the middle, like a talking head.

Step by step

  1. Trim your clip to the best 15 to 60 seconds in any editor.
  2. Open the Video Compressor & 9:16 Resizer and choose the clip.
  3. Pick 9:16 blur fill or 9:16 crop, and a resolution (720p or 1080p).
  4. Press Convert video. The browser plays the clip once and records the vertical version.
  5. Add captions with Auto Captions so it works with the sound off.

Before you post

  • Check that text and faces sit inside the safe zone, away from the buttons.
  • Start with your strongest line in the first second; vertical feeds are unforgiving.
  • Keep the file small enough for WhatsApp or email with the same tool's size targets if you share it directly.

Questions

What resolution should a Reel be?

1080 × 1920 is standard. 720 × 1280 is lighter and still looks good on most phones.

Will the quality drop?

Choose 1080p and the WhatsApp target only when you need a small file. For posting, a higher size target keeps more detail.
