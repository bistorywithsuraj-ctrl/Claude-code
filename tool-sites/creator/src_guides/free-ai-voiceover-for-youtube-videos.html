Not everyone wants to record their own voice, and studio-quality voiceovers used to cost money. Open-source AI voices are now natural enough for faceless channels, explainers and Shorts, and you can run one for free.

Step by step

  1. Write your script and check its length with the Script Timer.
  2. Open the AI Voiceover Generator and paste your script.
  3. Pick a voice: US or UK, male or female, calm or lively.
  4. Choose a speed and press Generate voiceover. The voice model downloads once, then works offline.
  5. Listen, then download MP3 or WAV and drop it into CapCut, Premiere or any editor.

Write for the ear

  • Short sentences. AI voices sound most natural with one idea per sentence.
  • Punctuation is direction. Commas add a breath and full stops add a pause. Use them on purpose.
  • Spell out tricky terms. Write "S R T" for initials and spell numbers the way you would say them.
  • Read it aloud once. If you stumble, the AI will too.

Finish the video

Once your voiceover is on the timeline, run the finished video through Auto Captions to add word-by-word captions. Faceless videos especially benefit, because captions carry the story when viewers watch without sound.

Can I monetise videos with an AI voice?

The Kokoro voice model used here is released under the Apache 2.0 licence, which allows commercial use. YouTube's rules focus on original, valuable content, so add your own research, visuals and editing. Follow each platform's AI disclosure rules.

Questions

Is there a character limit?

No. The voice runs on your device, so there are no credits or limits. Very long scripts are easier to generate in parts.

Does it support Hindi?

Yes. There are four Hindi voices (Ananya, Vaishnavi, Arjun and Rohan) that read Hindi, Hinglish and mixed English words.
