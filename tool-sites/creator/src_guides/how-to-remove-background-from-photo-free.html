A clean cut-out makes thumbnails pop and product photos look professional. You no longer need Photoshop: an AI model can do it in your browser in a few seconds.

Step by step

  1. Open the Background Remover.
  2. Choose your photo. Pick Fast for people and portraits, or Best for products, pets and other objects.
  3. Press Remove background. The first time, the AI model downloads once.
  4. Choose a new background (transparent, a colour, or a blurred version of the original).
  5. Download a PNG to keep transparency, or a JPG with a solid colour.

Use it for a YouTube thumbnail

The classic thumbnail recipe is a cut-out of your face or product on a bright, simple background with two to four big words. After removing the background, open the Thumbnail Text Maker, upload your PNG over a colour, and add bold text. Export at 1280 × 720.

Getting the cleanest edges

  • Use a sharp, well-lit photo. Blurry edges confuse any model.
  • Contrast helps: a subject that stands out from the background cuts out better.
  • For hair and fur, try the Best quality option.
  • Shrink the final image with the Image Resizer if a platform has a file size limit.

Is it private?

Yes. Many free background removers upload your photo to a server. Creator Bench runs the AI model on your own device, so your photo never leaves your browser.

Questions

Is the background remover really free?

Yes, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many photos you process.

Why is the first run slow?

The AI model downloads the first time (26 MB for Fast, about 220 MB for Best). After that it is cached and loads in seconds.
