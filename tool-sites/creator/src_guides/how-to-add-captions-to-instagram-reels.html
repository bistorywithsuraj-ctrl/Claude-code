Most people watch Reels with the sound off, so captions decide whether they keep watching. Here are three free ways to add them, from quickest to most eye-catching.

Why captions matter on Reels

Captions help viewers follow your video in quiet places, make your hook readable in the first second, and make your Reel accessible to people who are deaf or hard of hearing. Animated word-by-word captions also keep eyes on the screen, which is why so many viral Reels use them.

Option 1: Instagram's captions sticker

  1. Record or upload your Reel and tap Next to open the editor.
  2. Tap the sticker icon and choose Captions.
  3. Instagram transcribes your audio. Tap the text to fix mistakes and pick a style.

It is quick, but the styles are limited, it does not support every language well, and the captions only live inside Instagram. If you post the same video to TikTok or Shorts, you have to caption it again.

Option 2: AI auto captions you can reuse everywhere

The Creator Bench Auto Captions tool transcribes your video with AI right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no watermark.

  1. Open Auto Captions and choose your video.
  2. Press Generate captions. The first time, the AI model downloads once; after that it is fast.
  3. Fix any word in the list. The preview updates instantly.
  4. Pick one of 10 styles, such as Bold pop, Word box or Karaoke.
  5. Press Export captioned video to get a video with the captions burned in, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Option 3: Download an SRT file

If your editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve) supports subtitle files, download the SRT from the same tool and import it. YouTube also accepts SRT files in YouTube Studio under Subtitles.

Tips for captions that get watched

  • Keep lines short. One to three words at a time is easiest to read on a phone.
  • Stay in the safe zone. The like, comment and share buttons cover the right side and the bottom. Check your layout with the Safe Zone Checker.
  • Highlight key words. A different colour on the spoken word draws attention.
  • Proofread names and numbers. AI is good, but brand names and figures are where mistakes happen.

Questions

Do captions help Reels get more views?

Captions keep viewers who watch without sound, and longer watch time is one of the signals that helps a Reel reach more people.

Can I add Hindi captions?

Yes. In Auto Captions choose the Accurate model and pick Hindi, or leave it on automatic detection.
