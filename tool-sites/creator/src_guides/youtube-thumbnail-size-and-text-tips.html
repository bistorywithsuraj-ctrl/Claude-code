Your thumbnail and title are the whole sales pitch for your video. Here is the exact size YouTube wants and the text habits that make thumbnails readable on a phone.

The right size

  • 1280 × 720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio). The minimum width is 640 pixels.
  • Under 2 MB, as JPG, PNG or GIF.

The Image Resizer has a YouTube thumbnail preset that crops to 16:9, resizes to 1280 × 720 and compresses under 2 MB.

Text that reads at thumbnail size

  • Three to five words. Thumbnails are tiny in the feed. Say one idea, not a sentence.
  • Don't repeat the title. The thumbnail and title should work together, each adding something.
  • Use a thick, bold font with an outline or shadow so it stays readable on any background.
  • Highlight one word in a bright colour to guide the eye.
  • Leave the bottom-right corner clear. YouTube puts the video length there.

The Thumbnail Text Maker does all of this with ready templates: type your words, mark the word to highlight, drag it into place and download at 1280 × 720.

Make the subject pop

A face or product cut out from a busy background is easier to see. Use the Background Remover to get a transparent PNG, then place it on a bright colour.

Check it next to your title

Before you publish, preview the pair. The Title & Thumbnail Preview shows your thumbnail and title in the home feed, search and sidebar, in light and dark mode, and warns you where the title gets cut off.

Questions

What is the best thumbnail size for YouTube Shorts?

Shorts use a frame from the video by default. If you set a custom thumbnail, keep the important part in the centre so it works when cropped.

Why is my thumbnail blurry?

It is usually too small or over-compressed. Export at exactly 1280 × 720 and keep the file just under 2 MB.
